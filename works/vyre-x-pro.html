Titanium, not plastic.

Grade 5 titanium unibody machined to 0.02mm tolerance. The acoustic chamber is carved, not molded — eliminating resonance and adding structural rigidity you feel the moment you hold it.

  • CNC-machined from single billet
  • Ceramic bead-blasted matte finish
  • IPX7 — fully sealed, no mesh grilles

Adaptive ANC that listens.

Six microphones per side. Our H2 chip samples ambient noise 48,000 times per second and generates an inverse wave in real time. Not just quiet — absent.

  • -42dB broadband reduction
  • Wind-resistant beamforming mics
  • Transparency mode with natural EQ

Engineering notes

01 — ACOUSTICS

Carved chamber

Instead of plastic tubes, we machine the entire acoustic path from titanium. Result: zero unwanted resonance at 2-5kHz where vocals live.

02 — POWER

Silicon-carbon cell

New Si-C anode holds 18% more capacity in same volume. 8 hours real-world with ANC on, not lab conditions.

03 — CONNECTIVITY

Ceramic antenna

Laser-direct-structured antenna printed inside the titanium shell. Maintains signal through metal where others drop.
